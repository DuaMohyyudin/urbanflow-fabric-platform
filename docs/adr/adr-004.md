# ADR-004: Collapse exact duplicates on trip_id

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-07-29

## Context
The trip_id hash (9 business columns including signed total_amount and
payment_type) found one collision in 9.55M rows. Inspection showed two
byte-identical rows: the same trip recorded twice at source.

A separate finding: an earlier 7-column hash had collapsed 333 refund pairs
— a +N charge and its -N reversal at an identical timestamp — into single
ids. Adding signed total_amount to the hash separated them.

## Decision
Keep one row per trip_id via QUALIFY ROW_NUMBER(). Exact duplicates are
collapsed, not retained. Refund pairs are NOT collapsed — they differ in the
sign of total_amount and are distinct transactions.

## Consequences
- silver_trip + quarantine no longer equals source exactly; the conservation
  invariant accounts for collapsed duplicates explicitly (1 row).
- Distinct from ADR-003: quarantine retains bad-but-real rows; this removes
  rows that carry no information (a true duplicate).
- Establishes the principle that a business key must include the columns that
  make two rows genuinely different — for financial data, the signed amount.
