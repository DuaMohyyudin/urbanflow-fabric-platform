# ADR-005: Unknown dimension members and as-of SCD2

**Status:** Accepted · **Date:** 2026-07-29

## Context
Two Gold-layer facts needed a decision.
1. 18 trips carry pickup dates outside the modelled calendar (2002, 2008,
   2009, 2023). An inner join to dim_date silently dropped them.
2. The TLC zone lookup is effectively static, but SCD2 is a required
   capability to demonstrate for DP-600.

## Decision
- Every dimension carries an unknown member at surrogate key -1. Fact foreign
  keys that cannot resolve point to -1, never to NULL, and rows are never
  dropped.
- The 18 out-of-period trips route to date_sk = -1, preserving grain.
- fact_trip joins dim_zone as-of the trip's pickup date (valid_from /
  valid_to range), not on the current version.
- One synthetic change (zone 1 service_zone -> 'Airports (updated)',
  effective 2024-06-01) was injected to prove SCD2 tracks history. It is
  retained in the data intentionally.

## Consequences
- Grain is preserved: fact rows = silver rows (invariant G1 passes).
- Nothing is silently dropped — the Phase 2 principle extends to Gold.
- The as-of join is verified by money conservation (G5): fact totals match
  silver to the penny, proving no join duplicated or dropped rows.
- dim_zone shows two versions of zone 1; this is a demonstration, not real
  TLC history, and is documented as such.
- Because trip data is Jan-Mar and the synthetic change lands 2024-06-01,
  all current trips join the pre-change version — exercising the as-of path.
